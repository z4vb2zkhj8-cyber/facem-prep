FACEM Prep OSCE prototype. One educational adaptation of the user-provided 2025.1 OSCE 4 sample. 4-minute reading timer, 7-minute station, browser speech synthesis, browser-dependent speech recognition, local candidate audio recording, transcript JSON/audio downloads and manual rubric. No live AI API, no cloud recording or reviewer dashboard upload. Browser speech recognition may transmit speech to the browser provider and is not supported everywhere. For a production AI roleplayer and secure remote review, add a server-side real-time speech/LLM service, explicit recording consent, authenticated object storage, retention policy, and reviewer permissions. Medical wording should be peer-reviewed, particularly the source attribution of infection to partner and treatment recommendations. Do not upload actual patient information.
